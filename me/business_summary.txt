SunCedar Solar (fictional company) - Business Summary

SunCedar Solar is a Lebanese company, founded in 2021 and based in Zalka (Metn), that designs and installs solar power systems for homes. Our mission is to help Lebanese families stop depending on short EDL grid hours and expensive generator subscriptions (the "ishtirak") by giving them their own reliable, clean power on their own roof. We have completed more than 1,400 home installations.

We offer four services: complete home solar installations, battery and inverter systems (LiFePO4 lithium batteries with hybrid inverters, including upgrades of existing systems), SunCare maintenance plans, and a free, no-obligation site assessment by an engineer, usually within 48 hours of a request. We sell three fixed-price packages, all including installation, mounting, wiring, protection, EDL and generator integration, a monitoring app and one free maintenance visit in the first year: Basic ($3,450: 3.3 kWp, 5 kW inverter, 5.12 kWh battery, for apartments and small homes), Family ($6,450: 6.6 kWp, 8 kW inverter, 10.24 kWh battery, for 4 to 6 people, runs 1 inverter AC in sunny hours) and Villa ($12,900: 13.2 kWp, 12 kW three-phase inverter, 20.48 kWh battery, runs 2 to 3 inverter ACs in sunny hours). Add-ons include an extra 5.12 kWh battery ($1,350) and extra 550 W panels ($140 each). SunCare Basic costs $120 per year (2 visits) and SunCare Plus costs $220 per year (4 visits, battery health report, 24-hour priority technician).

What makes us different are three promises. First, a fixed-price quote after a free site visit: the price on the quote is the price you pay. Second, installation within 7 days of the deposit, or we credit $50 for every day of delay. Third, 24/7 WhatsApp support from real people at +961 70 000 111. Warranties: panels 12 years (product) and 25 years (performance, at least 80%), inverter 5 years, battery 10 years or 6,000 cycles, workmanship 2 years. Payment is by cash USD, fresh-dollar bank transfer or card, with a 30% deposit and 70% on completion; Family and Villa can also be paid 40% upfront plus 6 monthly installments at 0% interest.

We serve Beirut, Mount Lebanon (Metn, Keserwan, Baabda, Aley, Chouf), the North (Jbeil, Batroun, Koura, Tripoli), the South (Saida, Jezzine, Tyre), and Zahle and Chtaura in the Bekaa (with a $150 travel fee). SunCedar was founded by Karim Haddad (Chief Engineer, electrical engineer with 11 years in power systems), Rania Khoury (Head of Operations, who runs the warehouse and 6 installation crews) and Maya Nasr (Head of Customer Success, who built the 24/7 WhatsApp support team).
